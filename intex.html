STAMINA / ENDURANCE

Foods that provide energy for running and longer sessions.

FOOD OPTIONS

  • Rice + dal
  • Roti + vegetables
  • Oats + banana + milk
  • Potatoes + eggs
  • Poha + vegetables
  • Idli + sambar
  • Upma + vegetables
  • Banana + toast

EXAMPLE DAY

  • Breakfast: Oats + banana + milk
  • Snack: Apple or orange
  • Lunch: Rice + dal + vegetables + curd
  • Pre-training: Banana + toast
  • After training: Milk + banana
  • Dinner: Roti + dal + vegetables + eggs
02

SPEED / ACCELERATION

Speed work needs enough overall energy and regular meals.

BEFORE SPEED TRAINING

  • Banana + milk
  • Banana + toast
  • Oats + banana
  • Poha + curd
  • Idli + sambar
  • Roti + egg

AFTER SPEED TRAINING

  • Rice + dal + vegetables
  • Eggs + roti + vegetables
  • Chicken + rice + vegetables
  • Paneer + roti + vegetables
  • Curd + fruit + oats
  • Milk + banana + normal meal later

FOOTBALL TRAINING DAY

A simple example of meals around a football session.

BREAKFAST

Oats + Banana + Milk

LUNCH

Rice + Dal + Vegetables + Curd

Banana + Toast

AFTER TRAINING

Milk + Banana

Rice + dal + vegetables is another option.

EVERYDAY FOOTBALLER PLATE

Use familiar foods and build balanced meals.

🍚

ENERGY FOODS

Rice, roti, oats, potatoes, poha, idli, upma, bread and fruits.

🥚

PROTEIN FOODS

Eggs, dal, rajma, chana, milk, curd, paneer, chicken and fish.

🥦

FRUITS & VEGETABLES

Banana, apple, orange, mango, papaya, spinach, carrots, peas and other vegetables.

💧

FLUIDS

Water regularly throughout the day, especially around football sessions.

SIMPLE RULES

01

Do not skip regular meals just because you trained.

02

Eat a variety of foods instead of relying on one food.

03

Drink water regularly throughout the day.

04

Eat something familiar before training.

05

After training, have a normal balanced meal or snack.

06

Food needs can change with age, growth and training load.

ELITE FOOTBALL HQ NOTE: This is general football nutrition guidance, not a personalised diet. Young athletes are still growing, so regular balanced meals and enough food are important.
